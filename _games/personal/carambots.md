Carambots is a top-down action game created in collaboration with a team of game developers.
Inspired by the game [Carambola], each turn, you launch your bot like a billiard ball, sliding across the arena while trying to destroy your enemies. Shoot, bounce, and choose your path wisely to defeat your opponents and be the last bot standing.  

The game features multiple levels across different worlds, a variety of weapons, and several game modes. It also boasts a unique visual style and an exciting soundtrack.

***

## My Contribution

I served as the lead programmer and was responsible for the entire development process of the project.  
My responsibilities included implementing game mechanics, developing tools to assist other developers, integrating controls and assets, creating various systems, and optimizing the overall performance of the game.
