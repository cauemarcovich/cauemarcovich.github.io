**Crypt Raider** is a first-person exploration game where you enter a dark crypt. Your goal is to explore, solve puzzles, and find a legendary artifact hidden inside.  

The game has a dark atmosphere, with lots of mystery and suspense.

***

## My Contribution
Crypt Raider was made entirely by me as part of a course from **[GameDev.tv]**. It’s my first game using Unreal Engine, and I worked on it while getting familiar with the engine.

***

## Key Features
- Puzzles
- Dark and tense atmosphere
- Appealing graphics
