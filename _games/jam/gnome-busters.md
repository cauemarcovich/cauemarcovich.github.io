**Gnome Buster** was a game created for the [**GameDev.tv**][gamedevtv] jam (**2025**).
It’s a top-down game where the player must find a specific gnome hiding in one of many houses.

***

## My Contribution
I worked as the producer and lead programmer on Gnome Buster, managing the team, implementing some gameplay features like animations, and designing the project's code architecture.

***

## Key Features
- Charming unique art.
- Fun and catchy music.
- Lots of gnomes running around.
